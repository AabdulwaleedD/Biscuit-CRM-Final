BISCUIT CRM - QR CAMERA SCANNER

The Product Scanner uses Streamlit camera_input plus OpenCV QRCodeDetector.

FIRST TIME:
1. Run SETUP_SCANNER.bat (preferably from Anaconda Prompt).
2. Wait for opencv-python-headless and numpy to install.
3. Run RUN_CRM.bat, or use: streamlit run app.py

TEST:
Product Scanner -> Scan QR -> allow camera access -> scan a printed QR.

The scanner identifies the Product ID. It does not deduct stock during a simple scan.
Warehouse Release, Sales deduction, and Goods Receiving remain transaction-controlled workflows.
